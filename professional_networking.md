**S – Situation:**
Within my studies on career development, I studied an in-depth module of **Professional Networking** where the focus was on an understanding of how effective professional networks are a catalyst for personal growth, career growth, and long-term success. The module introduced me to key principles of effective networking, including communication, developing relationships, and business etiquette.

**T – Task:**
The task was to complete an **online quiz** that would assess my knowledge of networking concepts, such as the purpose of professional networking, why attending events is important, and how to employ the right approach while networking and maintaining professional contacts. The expectation was to develop a good understanding of how networking affects career as well as personal development.

**A – Action:**
Throughout the quiz, I reflected and answered several of the most significant questions, including:
- The primary purpose of professional networking is establishing valuable connections that lead to professional opportunities, mentorship, and the sharing of knowledge.
- A networking session is typically a professional conference—either online or offline—where individuals assemble to share experiences, ideas, and career guidance.
- The "golden rule" of networking is to give before you get by offering assistance, advice, or useful information before you expect any payback in return.
- Perhaps the most frequent error to avoid in business networking is failure to follow up after meeting someone new because this will dilute future possibilities.
- The first thing in building a professional network is specifying career goals and targeting people who have shared interests or work in the same industry.

**R – Result:**
I gained a better understanding of how to build and maintain professional relationships with intent and thoughtfulness through this module. I learned that networking is not solely about personal advancement but also about building a group of support to facilitate mutual growth and sharing. This exposure has increased my assertiveness at work, improved my skills in effective communication with colleagues and mentors, and empowered me with skills to build long-term working relationships that will aid me in advancing my ICT career and beyond.
