**S – Situation:**

I have completed a **mock interview** as a component of my career readiness to rehearse answering types of questions that I might encounter during professional job interviews. The session covered personal introductions, behavioral questions, technical knowledge, and problem-solving scenarios.



**T – Task:**

The task was to carry out the mock interview and demonstrate my communication skill, specialist knowledge, and critical thinking under a stressful condition. The plan was to assess my performance, identify good points, and improve weak areas where I could present myself confidently.



**A – Action:**

In carrying out the mock interview, I responded to questions of different types:



**1. Personal and Motivational Questions**

- I showed my enthusiasm for people connection and good experience creation through service and teamwork.

- I introduced myself confidently, highlighting my background in hospitality, communication skill, and eagerness to learn.

- In my worst weakness, I explained doing too much, and how I learned to delegate tasks.



**2. Questions Showing Competences**

- I explained how my communication skills allow me to build relationships and succeed in teams.

- I mentioned knowledge of Figma UX/UI design and Microsoft Office software and emphasized adaptability to new systems.

- I outlined how I deal with stress by being calm, prioritizing, and communicating to the team.



**3. Qualification-Based Questions**

- I mentioned certifications obtained from LinkedIn and expressed enthusiasm for continuous professional development.

- I detailed formal UX/UI design studies and informal hospitality training through previous work experience.

- I mentioned my current level of education, which is a diploma in ICT: Applications in Development, and my NSC matric certificate.



**4. Behavioral Questions**

- I provided examples of selfless or risky decisions, such as going home early to allow a co-worker to earn some extra money.

- I explained how I deal with pressure by communicating, planning, and prioritizing things that I have control over.

- I explained how I plan my goals by making large tasks into smaller manageable steps and reviewing progress on a regular basis.

- I gave an example of how to achieve a team goal: building a working website prototype with Figma and collaborating with my team.



**5. Technical Questions**

- I gave examples of challenges the field is facing, e.g., keeping up with technology without sacrificing user-centered practices.

- I explained my vision for the field's future as a mix of technology and empathy for better user experiences.

- I discussed software I utilize for UX/UI design (Figma, feedback forms, analytics) and in hospitality (communication and collaboration).



**6. Brainteaser Questions**

- I explained to a blind person a sunset as a receding warmth and peaceful hour, illustrating the use of descriptive, sensory language.

- I explained why manhole covers are round, with safety, longevity, and ease of use as reasons.

- I completed the probability of obtaining a sum of 10 with two dice as 1/12, demonstrating mathematical sensibility and basic understanding of probability.



**R – Result:**

This helped me become more assured in responding to different kinds of questions, put my answers into experience frameworks, and even improved my critical thinking ability with a time limit. I have now learned how to present in a unique way, express my finest strengths in a clear manner, and demonstrate problem-solving and technical ability, all of which prepares me for real interviews in professional and technical environments.
