**S – Situation:**  
As part of my career preparation coursework, I completed a module on **Business Communication**, which focused on how to communicate clearly, professionally, and effectively in the workplace.  

**T – Task:**
The task was to complete an **online quiz** designed to test my understanding of the purpose of business communication, appropriate language use, tone, and professional etiquette in both written and virtual settings.  

**A – Action:**  
Through the quiz, I answered questions such as:  
- The primary purpose of business communication is to share information clearly and achieve common goals.
- An example of non-verbal communication is body language, such as facial expressions or gestures.  
- Formal language should be used in professional settings like reports, emails to clients, or meetings with management.  
- The recommended tone for business emails is polite, respectful, and professional.  
- The proper etiquette for virtual backgrounds in video conferencing is to use a clean, neutral, and non-distracting background.

**R – Result:**  
I now understand how to communicate professionally in different business situations, choose the right tone and language, and maintain proper etiquette in both in-person and virtual environments. This has improved my ability to express ideas clearly and maintain professionalism in all forms of communication.
