**S – Situation:**
In the career development course, I undertook a module in **Workplace Etiquette** to familiarize ourselves with professional etiquette needed in today's workplace. The module educated us on communication, time management, confidentiality, professionalism, and respect in the workplace. The module was aimed at allowing the students to possess good professional image and understand how daily action impacts relationships and reputation in the workplace.

**T – Task:**
The activity was to complete an **online quiz** that would test my professional behavior skills in different work settings. These were how to handle confidential communication, proper use of personal devices, and meeting work obligations in respect and integrity. The emphasis was on performing well in projecting myself and my company positively while remaining ethical and considerate in processes.

**A – Action:**
I have responded to the following questions in the quiz and exercises:
- If I am receiving a **rude or offensive email**, I should not become angry, should not respond angrily, and settle the issue professionally or refer it to a supervisor if necessary.
- For a **10:00 AM meeting**, I must arrive at the meeting 5–10 minutes earlier than 10:00 AM to show respect for other people's time and to establish punctuality.
- For dealing with **confidential information**, I must maintain confidentiality, disclose it to authorized persons only, and never release it or via unsecured channels.
- It is **not professional** to receive non-work calls or send non-work messages on a personal cellular telephone at work because they are distracting and unprofessional.
- If I **must leave work early**, I must give my supervisor advance notice, give a brief reason, and ensure that all the necessary work has been done or left in charge.

These were steps in assuring that professional behavior is about more than the mere rules compliance—respect, responsibility, and care about how one's work affects the working environment are what it is about.

**R – Result:**
I now understand the significance of practicing good workplace etiquette to establish trust, promote teamwork, and project a professional image. From this module, I came to understand the significance of projecting professionalism in small daily things such as being respectful with time, respectful communication, and being efficient with time. I learned how to deal with adversity positively, respect confidentiality, and represent my organization and myself respectfully.

Generally, it made me feel more confident in being boldly more assertive when I interact in the workplace in order to be positively contributing to a positive, respectful, and productive workplace.
