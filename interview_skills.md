**S – Situation:**  
As part of my career preparation coursework, I completed a module focused on **Interview Skills**, which covered how to prepare for interviews, answer questions effectively, and present myself professionally.  

**T – Task:**  
The task was to complete an **online quiz** designed to assess my understanding of the purpose of interviews, company research, and the best answering techniques such as the **CAR** and **STAR** methods.  

**A – Action:**  
Through the quiz, I answered questions such as:  
- The primary purpose of a job interview is to determine if a candidate fits the role and company culture.  
- The **CAR (Context, Action, Result)** method helps structure responses clearly and effectively.  
- Researching a company before an interview shows genuine interest and helps tailor responses.  
- The best way to answer “**Tell me about yourself**” is by summarizing relevant experience, key skills, and career goals.  
- When asked about weaknesses, the best approach is to identify an area for growth and explain how I’m improving it.  

**R – Result:**  
I now understand how to prepare thoroughly for interviews, communicate confidently, and use the **STAR** or **CAR** methods to structure my answers. This has strengthened my ability to present myself professionally and increased my confidence for future job opportunities.
